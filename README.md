# av_categorical_perception_1c

**Research aims**

To verify the representativeness of sound of 3D objects categories.

**Method and Procedure**

The study is develop in PsychoPy (version 2022.2.5; Peirce et al., 2019) and it is conduct online via Pavlovia and Prolific.

[Peirce, J. W., Gray, J. R., Simpson, S., MacAskill, M. R., Höchenberger, R., Sogo, H., Kastman, E., Lindeløv, J. (2019). PsychoPy2: experiments in behavior made easy. Behavior Research Methods. 10.3758/s13428-018-01193-y]

The study is a two levels auditory identification task. Participants are before exposed to a training phase (to familiarise with the procedure) and a testing phase.
The total duration of the experiment was around 10 minutes.

**Stimuli**

The experiment presents sounds of familiar objects and their corresponding 3D objects shapes.

_3D Visual stimuli_ : The 3D objects shapes are created though the software SketchUp Pro (www.sketchup.com).

_Sounds stimuli_: A total of 16 sounds were tested during the study, each sound was representing an exemplar of object categories (hand bell, church bell, beer glass, wine glass, single stem vase, urn vase, wine bottle, coke bottle). All the sounds had a duration of 500 ms and a rate of 44100 Hz, duration and rate of the auditory stimuli was kept constant thought version 3.0.0 of Audacity®.

**Design**

The experiment is a mixed design, with object type (bell, glass, vase, bottle) as within factor and version (images or labels) as between factor.

**Dependent variables**

Accuracy and RTs.

**Independent variables**

Object type, version and their interaction.

**Covariates**

Age, sex, and object type.

**Ethics**

Full ethical approval was obtained from the School of Psychology Ethics Committee, Trinity College Dublin.
